Overview

BUILT TO WITHSTAND THE ELEMENTS.

Your home's exterior takes on sun, salt air, wind, and moisture year-round. A quality exterior paint job is protection first, aesthetics second — that's why we invest as much attention in preparation as we do in the finish coat.

What's Included

EVERY EXTERIOR PROJECT COVERS THE DETAILS.

Surface Repair

Repair of cracks, dents, and damaged stucco or siding before painting begins.

Sanding & Scraping

Removal of peeling or failing paint for a clean, adhesive-ready surface.

Priming

Targeted priming on bare or repaired areas for even, long-lasting coverage.

Weather-Resistant Coatings

Premium exterior paints selected for durability against sun and moisture.

Trim & Detail Work

Clean lines on trim, fascia, and architectural details.

Final Inspection

A thorough walkthrough to confirm quality before we consider the job complete.
